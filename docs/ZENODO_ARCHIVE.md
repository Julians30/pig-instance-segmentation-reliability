## Access boundaries

Original FaroPigSeg images, annotations and ground truth are not redistributed; obtain them from the original dataset custodians, respecting CC BY-NC 4.0. Full checkpoints, complete prediction JSON files and ancillary analysis workbooks are not included. Available study-generated inputs may be requested from the corresponding author, subject to applicable third-party terms. A full computational reproduction requires obtaining or regenerating the documented inputs. Original project code and documentation remain MIT; third-party terms are unaffected.
